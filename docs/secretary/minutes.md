# Meeting Minutes

## Record the meeting

1. Bring the current agenda and the previous meeting's minutes.
2. Take notes against the agenda during the meeting.
3. For each motion, record:
   - Who made the motion.
   - Who seconded the motion.
   - Whether the motion carried or did not carry.
4. Capture meeting reports and decisions accurately without treating an unconfirmed proposal as an approved action.

## Prepare the minutes

The [Secretary Responsibilities Cheat Sheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/165qGju1ZPAULRnlNKMzcoTDUj_ZjKSPmxse2m5eU7A0/edit) suggests using the annotated agenda as the basis for the minutes and renaming it accordingly. Preserve the agenda template and prior records when doing so.

Store the resulting minutes with the club's meeting documents on Google Drive. The next meeting reminder should link to the previous meeting's minutes.

!!! note "Procedure still to confirm"

    The source does not specify a formal review, correction, approval, or publication workflow for minutes. Document those steps after the club confirms its practice.
