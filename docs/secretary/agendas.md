# Meeting Agendas

## Prepare the agenda

1. Find the current agenda template in the club's Google Drive Agendas and Minutes folder, and make a copy for the upcoming meeting.
2. Ask officers and committee chairs for agenda input in the officers' Facebook Messenger chat. Examples include the Treasurer's report and upcoming service opportunities.
3. Include a review/approval item for the **previous meeting's draft minutes**. Link the previous meeting's working Google Doc so members can review it before voting; it is not yet the finalized PDF.
4. Incorporate confirmed reports and meeting items into the new agenda.
5. Check the meeting date, time, location, and remote-attendance information before distribution.

## Share the agenda

Include a direct link to the agenda in the meeting announcements sent to the club Google Group. See [Meeting Lifecycle and Announcements](meeting-lifecycle.md) for suggested timing, recipients, reminder content, and link checks.

## Prepare printed copies

Print and bring copies of the agenda and previous meeting's draft minutes for review and approval. The source cheat sheet suggests coordinating with members who have access to free printing to avoid personal expenses; confirm current availability rather than assuming it.

## Related procedure

During the meeting, use the agenda to take notes and prepare the [Meeting Minutes](minutes.md).
