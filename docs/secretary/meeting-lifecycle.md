# Meeting Lifecycle and Announcements

This procedure connects [agenda preparation](agendas.md), meeting announcements, the meeting itself, and [minutes](minutes.md).

## Suggested timeline

The timing below is **guidance, not a strict deadline**. Adjust for holidays, changes in meeting plans, or the availability of materials.

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Before the first announcement | Prepare the agenda, request officer and committee reports, and verify meeting details. |
| Roughly one week before | Send the main meeting announcement to the club Google Group. |
| Roughly one day before | Send a short reminder to the same group, highlighting any changes and linking to the agenda. |
| At the meeting | Bring agenda and prior minutes; record proceedings and motions. |
| After the meeting | Prepare minutes for the club's established review process and retain them with meeting records. |

## Send announcements through the club Google Group

Use the club mailing list **SFFilAmLions@googlegroups.com** for general meeting announcements. Confirm the current list and distribution expectations before sending.

### Main announcement

Include:

- Meeting date, start time, and physical location.
- Verified remote-attendance instructions, including the Zoom link when applicable.
- A direct Google Drive link to the upcoming agenda.
- A direct Google Drive link to the previous meeting's minutes.
- Any important attendance or participation instructions confirmed for that meeting.

Check that the linked documents are the correct versions and accessible to intended recipients. The [Secretary Responsibilities Cheat Sheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/165qGju1ZPAULRnlNKMzcoTDUj_ZjKSPmxse2m5eU7A0/edit) provides an example message, but its location and Zoom details must be verified for each meeting.

### Follow-up reminder

Around the day before the meeting, send a concise reminder to the Google Group. Repeat the date, time, location, remote-attendance instructions, and agenda link. Highlight changes to the meeting plan or materials since the first announcement. Link the previous minutes when useful.

The two-message pattern is a **recommended default**, not a requirement to send on exact dates. Avoid unnecessary duplicate messages if a meeting is rescheduled or an announcement was already sent close to the meeting.

## At and after the meeting

Use the [Meeting Minutes](minutes.md) procedure to capture reports, motions, seconders, and outcomes. Store minutes with the club's meeting records so they can be linked in the next meeting announcement.
