# Secretary

This section documents the club Secretary's meeting preparation and minutes procedures.

- [Meeting Lifecycle and Announcements](meeting-lifecycle.md) — coordinating preparation and member emails through the club Google Group.
- [Meeting Agendas](agendas.md) — creating and updating the agenda.
- [Meeting Minutes](minutes.md) — recording meeting proceedings.

These procedures are based on the club's [Secretary Responsibilities Cheat Sheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/165qGju1ZPAULRnlNKMzcoTDUj_ZjKSPmxse2m5eU7A0/edit) and discussion of the club's preferred announcement cadence. Verify meeting details, links, and recipients against current club information before sending or publishing anything.

Adding new members is a planned topic for separate discussion; no procedure is documented yet.
