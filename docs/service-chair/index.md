# Service Chair

The Service Chair leads the San Francisco Fil-Am Lions Club's service activities, coordinates volunteer opportunities, and ensures that the club's service impact is accurately reported to Lions Clubs International.

## Responsibilities

- **Service Planning:** Develop annual service goals and identify community needs in coordination with club leadership.
- **Event Coordination:** Organize and oversee service projects, including partnerships with community organizations.
- **Volunteer Engagement:** Encourage Lions, Leos, youth, and community members to participate.
- **Club Collaboration:** Coordinate with the President, Secretary, Marketing team, Membership Chair, and other committees.
- **Service Reporting:** Ensure completed activities and their impact are recorded accurately in Lions Portal.
- **Meeting Reports:** Provide updates on upcoming activities, completed projects, volunteer participation, and service accomplishments.

## Managing Service Activities

The Service Chair coordinates service projects from planning through completion:

1. Identify opportunities addressing community needs and the club's service goals.
2. Confirm event details, volunteers, partners, and required resources.
3. Promote opportunities and coordinate volunteer participation.
4. Monitor progress and communicate significant changes.
5. Collect attendance and service impact information after each activity.
6. Report completed activities and share results with club leadership.

## Lions Portal — Service Reporting

[Lions Portal](https://lionportal.org/) is Lions International's platform for club service reporting. The Service Chair ensures activities are reported, either directly or through another authorized club officer.

### Reporting Responsibilities

For each completed service activity:

1. Collect the date, location, nature, and other relevant event details.
2. Record verified volunteers, people served, and volunteer hours, as applicable.
3. Document project outcomes and service impact.
4. Supply other applicable information requested in Lions Portal.
5. Submit or coordinate submission of the activity report.
6. Review recorded information for completeness and accuracy.

Report activities promptly after completion. The Lion Year runs from July 1 through June 30; prior-year service reporting is generally accepted through July 31, subject to Lions International's current reporting rules.

**Use verified participation and impact figures, not event registration counts as proof of attendance or completed service hours.**

## Coordination and Meeting Reports

Provide club meeting updates on:

- Upcoming service opportunities and volunteer needs.
- Recently completed activities.
- Verified volunteer participation and service impact.
- Progress toward annual service goals.
- Outstanding Lions Portal service reports.

Coordinate with the President or Secretary when assistance with reporting is needed.
