## Opening

> Good evening fellow lions and guests, it is now 7:00 PM.
>
> I call this meeting into order ( hit the bell with the gavel)
>
> May I  call Lion _______________ to lead  the Pledge of Allegiance. Thank you Lion _________ .
>
> May I call Lion   _________________to do Invocation. Thank you
>
> Lion_____________.
>
> Let us do our self introductions starting from my right.
>
> Please state your name and current position.(When everyone is finished with their self introduction, introduce yourself). I am Lion Romy Sepulia, your club president.
>
> Let us have our dinner and enjoy our fellowships. We will resume our meeting after the dinner. ( hit the bell with the gavel).

## AFTER DINNER……………………………………………

> Let us resume our meeting ( hit the bell with the gavel).
>
> May I call Lion PDG Dr, Jun Valera for the Secretary’s Report.

### ( After Secretary’s Report)

> Any motion to accept the Secretary’s Report?
>
> Lion ___________ moved to accept the Secretary’s Report.
>
> Any second on the Secretary’s Report.
>
> Lion _______________seconded the Secretary’s Report
>
> Any Discussion?
>
> Those who are in favor, say AYE! ( AY)
>
> Those who are not in favor, say NAYE ( NEY)
>
> The Aye have it, motion is approved to accept the Secretary’s Report.
